# Review the assembled draft

Review the complete assembled draft against the complete source, template, analysis mapping, and
section drafts. Identify omitted source details, unsupported claims, sections that are inadequate
or overly summarized, unresolved template requirements, duplicate content or questions, and
readability problems.

When a correction is necessary, return a complete corrected `DraftSection` that preserves source
grounding and existing fixed template text. Corrections may improve completeness, wording,
organization, and deduplication, but may not add new business information. Do not rewrite sections
that already meet the standard.
